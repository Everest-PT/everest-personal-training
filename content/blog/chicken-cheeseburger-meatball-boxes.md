Family meal prep usually fails on the same point: the adults will eat it and the kids will not. This one comes out of a single tray and gets eaten by both, which is the only test that matters on a Sunday afternoon.

Five servings, roughly **43g of protein** each.

## Why this one works

**High protein and family-friendly at the same time.** Chicken mince carries the protein without tasting like a diet decision, and shaping it into meatballs turns it into something children recognise as food rather than as an ingredient.

**It supports muscle repair and recovery.** 750g of mince across five boxes is a serious protein dose, and the cheese and egg add a little more.

**It reheats and travels.** The meatballs and roasted kumara hold up in a container all week, and the salad stays crisp because the dressing goes on at the end rather than at the start.

## A note on the macros

Approximate, per serving across five containers. Kumara instead of potato, more cheese, or a heavier hand with the oil will all move them. Treat them as a good estimate.
