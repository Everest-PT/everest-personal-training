Fried chicken is not the enemy of a training diet. The fryer is. Coat the same chicken in crushed cornflakes and bake it, and you keep the crunch while the fat stays in single-digit territory per serving.

Five servings, roughly **50g of protein** each.

## Why this one works

**Lean, high-protein and family-friendly.** 750g of chicken breast across five boxes, with the coating adding a little extra from the parmesan and egg rather than a pile of oil.

**Wholemeal wraps and vegetables support energy and fibre intake.** At 8g of fibre per serving this is well ahead of most meal prep, which matters for fullness as much as digestion.

**The Greek yoghurt sauce boosts protein without heavy sauces.** Mayonnaise-based dressings can quietly add 15 to 20g of fat to a meal. Building the sauce on yoghurt with a spoon of mayo for flavour gets you the taste at a fraction of that.

**It is built to survive the week.** Keeping the wrap out of the box until serving is the single detail that makes this work on day four.

## A note on the macros

Approximate, per serving across five containers. Wrap size varies a lot between brands and is the biggest single variable here — check the packet if the carbohydrate figure matters to you.
