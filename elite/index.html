What is Everest Elite?

Everest Elite is the performance arm of Everest Group. It has two arms: Everest Athlete, which delivers sport-specific strength and conditioning to athletes, teams and squads, and Everest Executive, which delivers physical and mental performance coaching to executives and high-performing individuals. Both are scoped and priced per case rather than sold at a fixed weekly rate.

What is the difference between Everest Executive and Everest Organisations?

Everest Executive coaches one person: an executive, founder or leader, on their own physical and mental performance. Everest Organisations delivers workforce programmes across a whole team or business. If you are looking after yourself, that is Executive. If you are looking after your staff, that is Organisations.

Do you offer strength and conditioning in Christchurch?

Yes. Everest Athlete delivers sport-specific strength and conditioning in Christchurch and across Canterbury, in person and online, for individual athletes as well as whole teams, squads and clubs.

Do I need to be an elite athlete?

No. Everest Elite suits any committed person chasing a measurable performance goal, whether that is a developing athlete, an experienced competitor, or an executive whose performance depends on their energy, focus and resilience holding up under load.

How much does Everest Elite cost?

Everest Elite is priced per case. Scope depends on testing, contact hours, whether delivery is individual or squad-wide, and the length of the block. You receive a written proposal with the price before anything begins.

Do you work with teams and clubs?

Yes. Everest Athlete coaches whole teams, squads and clubs, with consistent testing, programming and load monitoring built around your competition calendar.
