What is EMPOWER

Big skills, built one session at a time

EMPOWER teaches children important life skills through the lens of movement, health and wellbeing. Each session combines guided physical activity with age-appropriate learning to help children better understand their bodies, their minds and how to take care of both.

Move well

Physical literacy, coordination, strength foundations and safe movement habits.

Feel strong

Confidence, emotional awareness, self-belief and resilience through challenge.

Thrive together

Teamwork, communication, leadership and positive social connection.

Why it matters

Building healthy habits before problems begin.

Many children are growing up with less movement, more screen time and increasing pressure on their physical and emotional wellbeing. EMPOWER supports children early by giving them the tools, confidence and habits to engage with movement positively for life.

Exercise should not only be used after a problem appears. EMPOWER supports a preventative approach by helping children develop capability, confidence and wellbeing habits early.

How sessions work

Five parts to every single session

EMPOWER sessions are structured, age-appropriate and designed to work in schools, after-school care environments, community programmes and sports settings.

1

Welcome & focus

A settling, focus-building activity to start each session positively.

2

Movement skill block

Guided physical literacy and safe movement skill development.

3

Strength or game

A strength, coordination or game-based challenge to build capability.

4

Wellbeing learning

An age-appropriate health or wellbeing learning point for the day.

5

Reflection & close

A reflective, confidence-building close to finish each session.

The partnership

Launching in partnership with Busy Bumbles

Busy Bumbles is a before and after-school care provider with 20 locations across the South Island, looking after tamariki beyond the school day with a focus on play, connection and genuine care.

EMPOWER is proud to launch its pilot programme in partnership with Busy Bumbles. They have shown real leadership in supporting children beyond traditional after-school care, which is what makes theirs the right environment to test EMPOWER with real children, families and staff.

Want to learn more about our incredible partners? Visit the Busy Bumbles website

Two organisations. One mission: helping every child thrive.

Pilot impact

Launching with measurable community impact

Running Wednesday afternoons from 21 October to 25 November 2026, the pilot works with 75 children across a 6-week delivery block. The data collected will help shape future EMPOWER delivery across schools, after-school care providers and community settings.

This pilot is designed to test EMPOWER in a real-world after-school environment and gather the outcome data required to refine the programme before future expansion into schools and wider community settings.

Measurable by design

A programme designed for measurable impact

EMPOWER is not designed as a one-off activity. It is being developed as a measurable youth development programme. During pilot delivery, we aim to track:

  • Attendance
  • Participation
  • Completion rates
  • Child enjoyment
  • Movement confidence
  • Physical literacy
  • Health and wellbeing knowledge
  • Parent feedback
  • Staff feedback
  • Safety and delivery quality
