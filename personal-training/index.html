Do you offer personal training in Christchurch?

Yes. Everest Personal Training offers in-person personal training across Christchurch and Rolleston, plus online coaching for clients anywhere in the world. One-on-one sessions are $90 per hour, or $800 for a ten-session card.

How much does a personal trainer cost in Christchurch?

At Everest, a one-on-one session is $90 per hour and a ten-session concession card is $800. Coached weekly programmes are $30 per week, personalised online coaching is $20 per week, and self-guided app programmes are $10 per week.

Can I do online personal training if I am not in Christchurch?

Yes. Online coaching delivers programming, accountability and feedback through the Everest app, with one-on-one consultations by video call. We currently coach clients in New Zealand, South Africa and Canada.

What is the difference between an app programme and personalised coaching?

An app programme gives you a structured plan to follow yourself, with no personalisation. Personalised coaching is built specifically around your goals, schedule and equipment, with regular check-ins, technique feedback and a coach who adjusts the plan as you progress.

Do you train beginners?

Yes. We coach people who have never set foot in a gym, people returning after a break or injury, and experienced lifters. Every programme starts from an assessment of where you actually are.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Programmes are built around the equipment you can actually access, whether that is a full gym, a home setup with minimal kit, or bodyweight only.
